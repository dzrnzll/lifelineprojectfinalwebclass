The story behind Lifeline AI

Our Mission

Lifeline AI was built to give people a fast, discreet way to reach help in moments that matter most. What started as a solo project has grown into an active platform used in real institutional safety pilots, built from the ground up over months of iteration.

The platform was recognized as the Red Bull Basement World Champion, beating out applicants from around the globe, and has since been featured in Forbes and adopted for a real-world pilot with a university department of public safety.
